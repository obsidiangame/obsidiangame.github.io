About Obsidian

Obsidian is a unblocked games website with over 200 games, with a simple tab cloak. Obsidian was started as a small project, shared between friends. Rebranded from the old UI. I saw Selenite, another game website, which was open source. I saw that the owner was trans so I decided to make my own game website not made by a trans person. So I took inspiration from them to build Obsidian.
